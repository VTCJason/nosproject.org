# About

## What this is

nOS Manifesto is a public-intelligence platform built around a central argument: that artificial intelligence represents a civilizational shift, and that most public conversation about it is inadequate to the scale of what is happening.

The platform has three components:

**The Manifesto** — a public declaration that names what is happening and calls for a particular posture toward it: honest about what is known, honest about what isn't, and committed to the long work of figuring out what to do about both.

**The Human Question** — a structured body of analysis across ten domains of human life, organized as a series of questions with honest verdicts on what the evidence currently supports.

**The Case Study** — a concrete examination of one specific pattern AI introduces: the redistribution of leverage between parties who were previously in a fixed relationship of dependency.
